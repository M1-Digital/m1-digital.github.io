## About M1 Digital

M1 Digital is a software development and research company specialising in blockchain technology and artificial intelligence. We design, build, and research the systems that power decentralised infrastructure and intelligent automation, working with organisations that want to put emerging technology into production.

### Our Mission

To advance how organisations build with blockchain and artificial intelligence by combining rigorous applied research — including Large Quantitative Models — with production-grade software engineering.

### Our Vision

To become a leading software development and research partner for blockchain and AI in Australia and the Asia-Pacific region, helping organisations move from prototype to production with confidence.
